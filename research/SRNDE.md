## Sound/Style Description
SRNDE operates in the melodic house and chill house space with crossover EDM-pop appeal. Tracks like "Missing" (Afro House Edit) show versatility across subgenres. The producer leans toward accessible, radio-friendly electronic music with strong streaming performance, released through established dance labels (Armada, Soave). The "BTM Chill Relax" series with one more cig suggests a downtempo/chillout side as well. SRNDE has also reimagined classic rock songs ("Eye of the Tiger," "I Was Made For Loving You" — KISS cover) with modern electronic arrangements.
