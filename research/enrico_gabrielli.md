# Enrico Gabrielli — Research Dossier

> Italian multi-instrumentalist, composer, and underground music linchpin. Born 1976 in Montevarchi, Italy. Also known as **Der Maurer**. A central node in Italy's experimental/library/psychedelic underground. Over 250 recordings in a 25+ year career.

---

## 1. Who Is Enrico Gabrielli?

- **Full name:** Enrico Gabrielli
- **Born:** 24 October 1976, Montevarchi (Arezzo), Tuscany, Italy
- **Based in:** Milan, Italy
- **Roles:** Multi-instrumentalist (clarinet, saxophone, bass clarinet, flute, keyboards, vintage analog synths, voice), composer, arranger, producer, writer, film/TV composer
- **Pseudonym:** Der Maurer; also uses "Enro Winston" in The Winstons
- **Training:** Diploma in clarinet, Conservatorio G. Verdi di Milano; studied composition. Studied with **Richard Stoltzman**, **Antony Pay**, and **Ciro Scarponi**.
- **Classical performance credits:** Works performed by **Orchestra del Teatro alla Scala**, **Fontana Mix** ensemble, **Arena di Verona** orchestra.
- **Recording output:** Contributed to **250+ recordings** over the past 25 years.
- **Also a novelist:** Debut mystery novel ***Virginia*** (Wudz Edizioni, 2024)

> *"A musician who has done a few things"* — his own self-description on Bandcamp.

## 2. Bands & Projects

### Calibro 35 🎸
- **Founded:** ~2007, Milan
- **Genre:** Cinematic funk, jazz-rock, psychedelic funk, library music, Morricone tribute, Poliziottesco-inspired
- **Members:** Enrico Gabrielli (sax, bass clarinet, flute, keyboards), Tommaso Colliva, Massimo Martellotta, Fabio Rondanini, Luca Cavina, Roberto Dragonetti
- **Key fact:** Their music sampled by **Dr. Dre** (*Compton*), **Jay Z**, **Child of Lov**, **Damon Albarn**
- **Olympics:** Performed at the **closing ceremony of the Milano-Cortina 2026 Winter Olympics**
- **Discography (11 studio albums, 4 live, 1 compilation, 4 EPs, 3 soundtracks, 20 singles — 2008–2024):**
  - *Calibro 35* (2008)
  - *Ritornano quelli di... Calibro 35* (2010)
  - *Traditori di tutti* (2013)
  - *S.P.A.C.E.* (2015)
  - *Decade* (2018)
  - *Nouvelles Aventures* (2023)
  - *Exploration* (2025)
  - *Ellroy vs L.A. (OST)* (2025/2026) — original soundtrack inspired by James Ellroy novels
  - *Blanca (Colonna Sonora Originale della serie TV)* (2021)
  - *Sandokan (Music from the Original TV Series)* (2021)
- **Film/TV scoring with Calibro 35:** *Blanca* (RAI series), *Sandokan* (TV series)
- **Official site:** [calibro35.com](https://www.calibro35.com/)

### The Winstons 🥁
- **Genre:** Progressive rock / Canterbury scene / psychedelic / chamber prog
- **Members:** Three "brothers" — Linnon Winston (drums), Rob Winston (bass), **Enro Winston** (Enrico Gabrielli — keyboards, woodwinds, vocals)
- **Discography:** *The Winstons* (2016), *Smith* (2020), *Third* (2024)
- **Prog Archives comparison:** "Van der Graaf Generator meets The Zombies"
- **Live recording:** *Montagnola Republic* (2020, Internet Archive)

### Der Maurer (solo project) 🎼
- **Enrico's deeply personal solo alias** for composed/chamber music works
- **Vol. 1** (2010): Clarinet, bass clarinet, double bass, piano — experimental acoustic chamber works
- **Vol. 2** (April 2025, 19'40"): Compositions written 1999–2025; miniature clarinet concertos, horn/violin/piano trios
- **Notable tracks:** *Corto Carminio* (2020) for clarinet, percussion, and samples; *Candzone* (2023) for two performers (LOLA DUO)
- **SoundCloud:** [soundcloud.com/dermaurer](https://soundcloud.com/dermaurer)
- **Vimeo promo:** [vimeo.com/12052298](https://vimeo.com/12052298)

### Mariposa 🦋
- **Genre:** Italian psychedelic funk
- Early project; discography includes *The Ol' Dirt Road* (2000)

### Esecutori di Metallo su Carta 🎻
- **Founded:** 2016, with Sebastiano De Gennaro
- **Genre:** Contemporary / experimental music ensemble
- Released *Progetto Generativo* — reworkings of Italian underground artists: **Zu, Aucan, Morkobot, Julie's Haircut, Hobocombo**

### 19'40" (recorded label / recording series) 📀
- **Founded:** 2016 by Gabrielli, Sebastiano De Gennaro, Francesco Fusaro
- **Concept:** "Where written music becomes a listening space" — a **subscription CD series**, one new release every 3 months
- **Described as:** "Anti-classical" recording series
- **At least 14 releases** (14th = *Ghosts Goblins Ghouls*, tribute to arcade game music)
- **Notable release:** *Mother Earth's Plantasia* (Mort Garson cover, 2019) — YouTube video with Enrico Gabrielli on keyboards, flute, clarinets, sax, voice; recorded at Laboratori Testone, Milan
- **Bandcamp:** [19m40s.bandcamp.com](https://19m40s.bandcamp.com/)
- **Website:** [19m40s.com](https://www.19m40s.com/)
- **Telegram news feed:** [t.me/s/open19m40s](https://t.me/s/open19m40s)

### Junkfood + Enrico Gabrielli 🎬
- **Collaborative project** with the Italian band Junkfood
- **Italian Masters** (2016): Reinterpretations of classic Italian film/library composers (Piero Umiliani, etc.)
- Recorded at **Vacuum Studio** (Bruno Germano / Iosonouncane) in Bologna
- Limited edition 300-copy multicolor vinyl (Cinedelic CNLP53)
- **YouTube:** [Junkfood + Enrico Gabrielli — Italian Masters Vol.1](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rVfW8CmKG4k)

### 4 Fiori per Zoe 🎞️
- **Collaborator** on *Musiche per film mai visti* (2010)
- Project featuring instrumental music composed for short films, indie features, and documentaries
- Other collaborators: Matteo Romagnoli, Nicola Manzanelli, Francesco Brini, Alessandro Grazian

## 6. Connection to Italian Underground / Lo-Fi / Vaporwave Scene

**Direct connections:**
- **Esecutori di Metallo su Carta** explicitly covers Italian underground artists: **Zu** (experimental rock), **Aucan**, **Morkobot**, **Julie's Haircut** (psychedelic pop), **Hobocombo** — a who's-who of Italy's experimental underground
- **19'40"** operates as a curatorial project bridging contemporary composed music and underground aesthetics
- **Junkfood** project revives obscure Italian library/vintage soundtrack material — the same pool sampled by lo-fi and vaporwave producers
- **Calibro 35's** library-music-as-original-composition approach feeds directly into the dusty/orchestral aesthetic prized by crate-diggers and sample-based producers

**Indirect but significant:**
- Vintage analog synth work, obscure Italian library music connections, cassette/CD-R limited editions
- **19'40" subscription model** mirrors the demotape ethos: small runs, direct-to-listener, curiosity-driven
- Bologna/Milan axis of experimental music (Vacuum Studio, Laboratori Testone) overlaps with Italian underground hubs

**No direct vaporwave/demotape output**, but his work exists in the same genealogical space that demotape culture draws from — obscure Italian composition, library music reissue, analog synthesis, small-batch physical releases.

## 7. Literary Work

### *Virginia* (2024)
- **Publisher:** Wudz Edizioni
- **Genre:** Mystery / noir
- **Setting:** An old fascist-era villa turned nursing home, inhabited by bizarre characters
- **Description:** A debut mystery novel set in the Valdarno area (Tuscany); the narrative weaves together eccentric characters in a gothic-tinged mystery
- **Reviews:** Off Topic Magazine praised its narrative craft and multi-layered storytelling
- **Context:** The novel reflects Gabrielli's broader artistic range beyond music — a "multiform talent"

## 9. Research Notes

**Why he matters for the Demotape project:**
- Enrico Gabrielli is a **hub figure** in Italy's experimental music network — connected to prog (Winstons), psychedelic funk (Calibro 35, Mariposa), contemporary classical (Esecutori, 19'40"), library music revival (Junkfood), and children's music (Le Canzonine).
- His **19'40" label** is a direct parallel to demotape culture: subscription-based, small runs, curatorially driven, bridging high-concept composition and underground aesthetics.
- The **Junkfood + Italian Masters** project is exactly the kind of obscure library-music archaeology that feeds sample-based/lo-fi scenes.
- His international collaborations (**PJ Harvey, Mike Patton, Muse, Iggy Pop, Afterhours**) place him in a network that bridges Italian underground and global alternative scenes.
- No direct connection to the cassette/vaporwave/demotape *format*, but his **aesthetic universe** (vintage synths, analog recording, Italian library music, obscure composition) is foundational to it.
