# Grand Transmitter — Research Report

**Band ID:** 28  
**Formed:** January 2003, London/Manchester, UK  
**Genre:** Atmospheric / Cinematic Indie Rock  
**Status:** Active 2003–c. 2005 (hiatus/disbanded after Everest era)

---

## 1. Biography & Origin Story

Grand Transmitter formed through a chance meeting in a Manchester bar in late 2002. Jake Fletcher (from Oldham, near Manchester) had spent 2002 writing and performing solo material. He met Tom — a resident DJ at the same bar — who played him demos and connected him with three London-based musicians (James "Pix" Pickering, John Hilton, Gavin Ahern) who had been searching venues and clubs for a singer after the collapse of their previous band. The four united in January 2003 and immediately began honing their sound: atmospheric, cinematic songs with Jake's soaring vocals and dark-edged lyrics.

The band's name evokes communication and broadcast imagery, though no explicit origin story has been documented.

## 3. Genre & Style

- **Primary:** Atmospheric indie rock, cinematic rock
- **Characteristics:** Soaring vocals (compared to a young John Lennon's "nasal croon"), dark-edged lyrics, orchestral string arrangements
- **Comparisons:** Spiritualized, Keane (bands they toured with)
- **Influences on production:** Later recordings with Mike "Prince Fatty" Pelanconi (dub/reggae producer) suggest an experimental direction

## 5. Label Information

### Faith & Hope Records (UK)
- **Owner:** David Wood (proprietor of Promenade Music shop)
- **Type:** Independent label, publishing & music synchronisation
- **Notable artists:** Mint Royale (big beat/electronic, #1 selling), Grand Transmitter
- **Status:** Ceased signing and releasing new material in 2007; continues to work back catalogue
- **Catalogue:** FHCD048 (Grand Transmitter – Under The Wheel EP)

### Ghost Records (Italy)
- **Founded:** 2002 in Varese, Italy
- **Founder:** Francesco Brezzi
- **Type:** Independent label & publishing (S.n.C)
- **Notable artists:** One Dimensional Man (historic Italian indie), Merci Miss Monroe, Grand Transmitter
- **Significance:** Grand Transmitter was Ghost Records' **first non-Italian band**
- **Address:** Via Sacco, 10 – 21100 Varese (VA), Italy

## 8. Interesting Trivia & Under-Researched Details

1. **First non-Italian at Ghost Records:** Grand Transmitter broke the Italian label's domestic-only pattern in 2004, the same year Ghost signed One Dimensional Man (a historic Italian indie act).

2. **The Galaxy Quartet:** The string quartet's name is reportedly a playful mash-up of their cellist Alex's name + "the gals" of the group (Alex + gals = Galaxy). They performed on both the debut EP and the unreleased Pelanconi sessions.

3. **Mount Everest Gig:** The band was confirmed for the "Everness Rocks" charity trek/concert at Everest Base Camp (May 2005), billed as "the world's highest gig" for the Nepal Cancer Relief Society. The Love Hope Strength Foundation organized similar treks in 2007 (Nick Harper participated in the 2007 edition).

4. **Mike "Prince Fatty" Pelanconi Connection:** The Anglo-Italian dub/reggae producer (known for modern dub production, VP Records, Evergreen Recordings, Lovedub Limited) was working with the band in early 2005 — an unusual stylistic pairing for an atmospheric indie rock band. This suggests a potential dub/experimental direction for their unreleased material.

5. **John Hilton's Design Work:** Bassist John Hilton designed the CD sleeve for the *Under The Wheel* EP.

6. **Jake Fletcher's Post-Band Career:** After Grand Transmitter, Fletcher became a well-known Manchester solo artist (album 2019, described as having a "cult following"), played guitar for The Specials, and was part of Manchester bands Gramotones and Cupids. His solo work has been praised for breaking free from the "solo male and guitar" cliché.

7. **James Pickering's Film Career:** "Pix" transitioned to film composition, scoring the BAFTA-nominated *County Lines* (2020, Lakeshore Records), with Mark Kermode calling his score "Majestic!" He also scored *Hello Carter* and *Snake Pit*.

8. **The Complete Short Stories:** After Grand Transmitter, all three instrumentalists formed this larger North London collective (7 members), suggesting they preferred collaborative, fluid band structures.

9. **Westside Studios:** The EP was recorded at the now-defunct Westside Recording Studios in Holland Park, London — founded and owned by Clive Langer & Alan Winstanley (producers for Madness, Elvis Costello, Morrissey, David Bowie, The Cranberries).

10. **Short but Impactful Output:** The band's confirmed released output totals roughly 13 minutes of music (one EP), yet they secured Glastonbury, BBC sessions, major tour support slots, and international label interest — a high impact-to-output ratio.
