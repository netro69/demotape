### Former/Early Members

The band evolved from "Precious Time" (school group) to Timoria in 1986. Early lineup changes occurred before their first major releases.

## 4. Collaborations & Notable Releases

### Antonella Ruggiero Collaboration (1997)
- Timoria collaborated with **Antonella Ruggiero** (ex-Matia Bazar) on her album *Registrazioni moderne*
- Tracks: "Ti sento" and "C'è tutto un mondo intorno"
- Released under Libera S.r.l. / BMG Rights Management Italy
- This is a significant connection linking Timoria to the broader Italian pop/rock scene

### Gianni Moroccolo (Producer)
- Former member of **CCCP Fedeli alla linea**
- Produced *Colori che esplodono* (1990)
- Important link between Timoria and the Italian punk/alternative legacy
