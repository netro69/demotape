# KEL (id=136) — Research Report

## Overview

**KEL** is the stage name of **Kelly Rose Moncado**, an Italian-Maltese electronic/dance vocalist and songwriter. Born to Maltese-Sicilian parents with German influences, she grew up in a culturally diverse environment that shaped her eclectic musical style — blending Mediterranean traditions, Eastern influences, and modern electronic production. She began singing at age four and has built an international career as a featured vocalist and topliner for major EDM producers.

- **Stage Name**: KEL
- **Real Name**: Kelly Rose Moncado
- **Genres**: EDM, Dance, Electronic, Pop, Electro House, Afro House
- **Active**: 2023–present
- **Label associations**: GEKAI, Dharma (Spinnin' Records), WMG (Warner Music Group)
- **Origin**: Malta / Italy (Maltese-Sicilian heritage)
