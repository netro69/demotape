## Overview

**Scisma** was an Italian **indie rock** band formed in **1993** on the western shores of **Lake Garda (Lago di Garda)**, in the province of **Brescia, Lombardy**. Active from **1993 to 2003**, with a brief reunion from **2014 to 2015**, they were a key act in the Italian alternative rock scene of the 1990s.

The band was described by critics as **"magniloquenti"** (magniloquent) and an **"imprescindibile"** (essential/indispensable) group for their sophisticated, refined, and intellectual approach to rock music. Their sound was rooted in rock but incorporated jazz, pop, European new wave, and experimental elements.

**Key facts:**
- **Formed:** 1993, Toscolano-Maderno area (Lake Garda west shore), Brescia, Italy
- **Genres:** Indie Rock, Alternative Rock, Art Rock, Chamber Rock, New Wave
- **Active:** 1993–2003, 2014–2015 (reunion)
- **Labels:** Self-produced (1995 debut), EMI/Parlophone (1997–1999)
- **Key venues:** Perpetuum Mobile Studios (Nave), 6th Floor Recording Studios (Brescia), Red Carpet Studios, Labuca (mixing)
- **Notable achievements:** Won **Rock Targato Italia** and **Arezzo Wave** in 1996, marking the beginning of their live career

## Musical Style & Influence

Scisma's sound evolved across their discography:

- **Bombardano Cortina (1995):** Raw indie rock debut with a DIY aesthetic, establishing their core sound of sophisticated, lyric-driven rock
- **Rosemary Plexiglas (1997):** Marked maturation — "an album very motivated" with more complex arrangements and production. Produced by **Manuel Agnelli** of Afterhours
- **Armstrong (1999):** Their artistic peak — described as "an hybrid between deviant pop and indie-line rock" with evident maturation in both lyrics and sound. Compared to Afterhours and Marlene Kuntz as part of the Italian alt-rock vanguard. Critics consider it an **underrated gem** of 90s Italian rock
- **Mr. Newman (2015):** Reunion EP showing the band retained their compositional skill — described as "grandioso" (magnificent)

**Critical reception:**
- Called **"magniloquenti"** (magniloquent) for their intellectual, refined approach
- Considered an **"imprescindibile"** (essential) group in the Italian indie landscape
- Armstrong was described as redefining Italian 90s rock with varied sonorities and deep lyrics

## Notable Facts

1. **Paolo Benvegnù's Death:** The band's leader, guitarist, and vocalist died on **December 31, 2024, at age 59**. He was eulogized as a seminal figure of Italian alternative rock. Born in Milan, transplanted to Perugia. Benvegnù also pursued a solo career after Scisma's initial breakup in 2003, releasing albums that won both critical and public acclaim.

2. **Studio Heritage:** Their debut was recorded at **Ritmo & Blu Studio** in Pozzolengo, but later work was done at **Perpetuum Mobile Studios** in Nave — a studio also associated with Marco Tonincelli. The 6th Floor Recording Studios in Brescia also hosted Scisma sessions.

3. **International Co-writer:** "I'm The Ocean" features **Marc Huygens**, frontman of the Belgian band **Venus** — an unusual cross-border collaboration for an Italian indie act in the late 90s.

4. **Tungsteno's Poetry:** The single "Tungsteno" uses hermetic poetry to describe chemical reactions inside light bulbs, comparing them to emotional/social fascicles — exemplifying the band's intellectual lyricism.

5. **Late Recognition:** Armstrong is considered far more loved in retrospect than it was upon release — a cult classic of Italian 90s rock.

6. **Name Confusion:** Searching for "Scisma" can yield results for the **TOOL tribute band SCHISM** (note different spelling). These are unrelated.

7. **Omnibus Compilation:** The 2015 "Omnibus" box set compiled their work, serving as a limited-edition retrospective during their brief reunion.

8. **Paolo Fidanzati Connection:** The website paolofidanzati.it hosted an official Scisma biography/biography page, indicating connections to the Fidanzati musical family in Italy.

9. **Tribute Concert:** A memorial concert was held in Padenghe with Scisma performing as honored guests, celebrating Benvegnù's legacy and contributions to Italian indie music.
