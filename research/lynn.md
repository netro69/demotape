**Lynn** is an Israeli pop singer, songwriter, and producer. Active since at least 2015 (when she appeared as a featured vocalist on a Ghost Records release), her solo career began in 2016. She has ~200K monthly listeners on Spotify and maintains an active presence across streaming platforms, social media, and music press.
