## Bio / Description

Francesca Ani is a Tampa-based Christian Contemporary singer-songwriter who began performing at age 6. She initially performed country music before transitioning to Christian pop. In 2015, she won a Tampa Bay Talent Search, which helped launch her music career.

Her musical influences include **Lauren Daigle** and **Carole King**. She released her debut pop EP, *"Open Your Arms"*, in 2016. While her early work was secular pop, she felt called to use her musical gifts for ministry and now focuses on Christian Contemporary music and youth worship.

Beyond music, Francesca is deeply involved in Catholic ministry. She works at a Catholic radio station in Tampa and serves as a music missionary with **Catholic Mission Trips Inc.** She has led mission trips to Harlan, KY; Lake Charles, LA; and Gallup, NM (Zuni reservation). Her goal is to "convey the message of the gospel with light and joy for any listener no matter their faith."

She is described by Madden Levin of *Unclear Magazine* as "about to take the world by storm."
