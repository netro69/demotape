### Why Blue Vomit?

Most under-researched *foundational* Italian punk band with only 5 links. Active 1978-1983, they were the direct precursor to Nerorgasmo — one of the most important bands in Italian hardcore punk history. Part of the legendary "furious years" of the Torino punk scene that also produced Negazione, Declino, Nerorgasmo, Raw Power, 5° Braccio, Kollettivo, and Stinky Rats. Despite their cult status and historical importance, the database had only 5 links — a critical gap.

### Key Scene Connections

- **Nerorgasmo** — Luca "Abort" Bortolusso (vocals) and Simone Cinotto (guitar) left Blue Vomit to form Nerorgasmo in 1983. Nerorgasmo became one of the most iconic and influential Italian hardcore bands of all time.
- **Declino** — Some Blue Vomit members contributed to Declino's formation. Declino's "Mucchio Selvaggio" split with Negazione is one of the most important artifacts of Italian hardcore.
- **Negazione** — Shared Torino punk scene. "Tax" (Roberto Farano) played drums for Declino before guitar in Negazione.
- **Raw Power** — Shared Torino punk scene.
- **5° Braccio** — Predecessor band whose members formed Negazione.
- **Kollettivo** — Shared Torino scene. Appeared on same compilation tapes.
- **Stinky Rats** — Torino punk contemporaries.

### Hard-Won Knowledge

1. **No torrent sources found** — Blue Vomit is too obscure for mainstream trackers. RuTracker, TPB, 1337x all returned zero results.
2. **F.O.A.D. Records is the primary archive** — Multiple reissues of the complete discography on LP. The 2011/2021 represses include unreleased tracks not on the 2006 CD.
3. **The "unreleased" Torinoise track** — One iconic unreleased track only circulated on a few copies of "Torinoise" — it was NOT included in the master copy used for the 2006 CD release.
4. **Vivo In Una Città Morta (2012)** — 100 hand-numbered USB memory sticks with 7 tracks + video interview + videoclip + bio insert + pin + patch + sticker. Ultra-rare collector's item.
5. **Nerorgasmo is the direct continuation** — Luca "Abort" Bortolusso and Simone Cinotto literally went from Blue Vomit to Nerorgasmo. Blue Vomit's nihilistic sound ("Vivo in una città morta", "Non sopporto") anticipated Nerorgasmo's style.
6. **Luca Bortolusso died in 2000** — The frontman of Nerorgasmo (and former Blue Vomit member) died of heroin addiction.
7. **Enrico Falulera** — Blue Vomit's drummer has a profile on Kleisma confirming his role in the band and the 2011 F.O.A.D. reissue.
8. **Torino 198X and Torinoise** — Both legendary Torino punk compilations from Disforia/Ansia Tapes. Blue Vomit appeared on both.
9. **Maximum Rocknroll praised them** — Described Blue Vomit as "one of the most sincere, punk and nihilistic bands in the Turin early '80s scene."
10. **The "Mai Capirai" connection** — This song was later re-recorded by Nerorgasmo, further cementing the band lineage.

### Underground Status Assessment

**Blue Vomit is moderately well-documented on the surface web** — their complete discography has been reissued by F.O.A.D. Records, and streaming/archive sources are present. The underground potential lies in:

1. **Demo '83 original cassette** — Self-produced, likely very rare. No known digitized version on Internet Archive.
2. **Physical Torinoise tapes** — The 1982/1983 compilation tapes (Ansia/Disforia) are rare collector items.
3. **Original tape artifacts** — Physical copies of the original demo cassettes are ultra-rare.
4. **Vanchiglia '82 / Via Artom '83 live recordings** — Only partially released on the 2011/2021 F.O.A.D. LP. Full live archive could exist.
5. **TVOR fanzine coverage** — TVOR (the most important Italian HC fanzine) likely covered Blue Vomit, but not yet digitized.

**Verdict:** Well-preserved through F.O.A.D. reissues but the original demo tapes and early compilations are underground artifacts. The band's historical significance as the precursor to Nerorgasmo and a foundational Torino punk act is well-established.
