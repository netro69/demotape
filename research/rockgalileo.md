## Overview

Italian pop/rock band from Florence, active in the 1990s. Not strictly noise rock but part of the broader Florentine alternative scene (see "Connections") and commercially successful via Sanremo. Beat-influenced indie pop/rock with strong songwriting. Released material on RCA/BMG sublabels and the independent PPM imprint.

## Key Connections

### Scene / Collaborative

- **Biagio Antonacci** — Studio & tour collaboration from 1996 onwards; Saverio Lanza wrote songs with/played in Antonacci's band. **LONG-TERM ONGOING COLLABORATION** (still active).
- **Litfiba** — Gianmarco Colzi joined Litfiba in 2001 as drummer (after RockGalileo disbanded). Shared member connection. Both Florentine bands.
- **Diaframma / Pankow / IRA / Neon** — Same Florentine "wave" scene (per OndaRock, Firenze Today, Carlo Casale).
- **Piero Pelù** — Saverio Lanza collaborated with the Litfiba frontman.
- **Vasco Rossi** — Saverio Lanza credited as collaborator.

### Post-band projects

- **Pastis** — Video-art duo formed by brothers Marco Lanza (photographer) and Saverio Lanza (music). Active from ~2005. Collaborated with Irene Grandi on *Lungoviaggio* visual album (2022).
- **Saverio Lanza (solo)** — Released album *Reunion* (2025); active as sessionman, producer, and songwriter (wrote "Controvento" for Arisa's Sanremo win).

### Band rename

- According to carlocasale.it: the band later shortened name to **"Galileo"** after the late 1990s.
