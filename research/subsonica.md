## 5. Lost / Rare / Notable Releases

These are the most relevant items for the Demotape project (lost catalog, underground connections):

### 5.1 Original Self-Produced Demo (1996)
- **5 tracks**, self-released before signing with Mescal
- Used to secure recording contract with Mescal
- Extremely rare; circulated in Turin underground scene

### 5.2 "U.F.O." (1997/1999)
- Instrumental tracks from "Preso blu" single remixed in England
- Released on **Lacerba** (London label)
- DJs: **Resident Filters** and **Terminal Head**
- Charted at **#7 in DJ Magazine UK** Top 5
- Also appeared on *Anni Luce 1997-2017*

### 5.3 *SUBurbani 1997-2004* (2005) — ⚠️ UNAUTHORIZED
- Released by **Mescal** during label dispute, WITHOUT band consent
- Contains rarities, B-sides, remixes, demo versions
- Tracks include: "Albascura," "Istantanee," "Liberi tutti (Subsonica remix)," "Il mio DJ (Coccoluto remix)"
- Considered "bootleg" by band; rare collector's item
- Track "Aurora sogna" appears here

### 5.4 *Mentale Strumentale* (Recorded 2004, Released 2020) — 🔴 LOST RECORDING
- **Instrumental album** recorded December 2004 during Terrestre sessions
- Submitted to Mescal to fulfill contractual obligation
- **Rejected** by Mescal as "albums of just noise"
- Sat in archives for **16 years**
- Finally released July 2020 during COVID lockdown
- WIRED Italy called it "a recording that rises from the dead"
- Also called "Microchip Temporale's instrumental twin"

### 5.5 *Anomalia Subsonica* EP (2003) — 🔴 RARE
- **4 unplugged tracks**: "Sole silenzioso (unplugged)," "Mellow Mood (unplugged)," etc.
- Included ONLY with official biography book (*Anomalia Subsonica* by Paolo Ferrari, Giunti Editore)
- Never released separately
- Extremely rare; only available with book purchase

### 5.6 *Il diluvio remixato* (2011)
- Remix EP with 10 tracks
- Producers: **Maztek**, **Hangm_n**, **Inward**, others
- DnB / dubstep reinterpretations
- Digital-only release

### 5.7 "Il Terrazzo" — Early Project
- Referenced in some sources as early Turin project/space
- Connected to the band's origins and Piazza Vittorio scene

### 5.8 *Coi piedi sul palco* (1998)
- Live mini-CD
- Included with limited reissue of debut album December 1998
- Features cover of CCCP "Tu menti," radio program "Patchwork" recordings

### 5.9 "Non identificato" Instrumentals (1997)
- Two instrumental tracks attached to "Preso blu" single
- Originally conceived for industrial area redevelopment project
- Remixed into U.F.O. for UK release

## 7. Italian Underground Scene Context

### Turin (Torino) — The Creative Crucible
Subsonica emerged from Turin's post-industrial cultural explosion of the mid-1990s. Key factors:

- **FIAT factory closures** → industrial spaces repurposed for clubs, social centers, studios
- **Centro Sociale circuit** (Askatasuna, etc.) → alternative music infrastructure
- **Piazza Vittorio** → Subsonica's spiritual home; where they rehearsed (Casasonica studio)
- **The Murazzi** → embankment clubs where electronic music scene thrived
- **Radio Flash** → local radio that championed underground acts

### The "Cieli su Torino" Festival (Traffic Festival 2004)
- **July 8, 2004** — Piazza Castello, Turin
- **11,000+ attendees**
- Subsonica + Africa Unite + Linea 77 + Mau Mau + Madaski + Persiana Jones + Fratelli di Soledad
- 3-hour continuous jam session — bands collaborating, not just alternating
- Closed with **Alessandro Baricco** doing Moby Dick parallel
- Documented the peak of Turin's collaborative underground ethos

### Subsonica as Underground-to-Mainstream Bridge
- **1996**: Born in clubs, self-produced demo
- **1997**: Signed to Mescal (independent label)
- **2000**: **Sanremo Festival** with "Tutti i miei sbagli" — underground enters mainstream living rooms without compromise
- **MTV Europe Music Awards 2000**: Best Italian Act (Stockholm) — first of two wins
- Maintained underground credibility while achieving platinum sales
- Refused Festivalbar after Sanremo; returned to club circuit
- *Noisy Naples Fest* review: "Una band nata tra club, dub e notti metropolitane porta il buio in televisione"

### Key Quotes on Underground Identity
- "Per noi è nato tutto nei club. La nostra storia parte da li" (Subsonica)
- "Se non ci fossero stato via Po e piazza Vittorio, non ci sarebbero stati i Subsonica" (Boosta)
- "Torino è la città più underground d'Italia" (Max Casacci, Rolling Stone Italia, 2026)
- "The most important act to emerge from the Italian underground in the second half of the 1990s" (Amazon Music bio)

## 11. Research Notes & Demotape Relevance

### Priority Items for Preservation
1. **Original 1996 demo** (5 tracks) — self-produced, pre-Mescal; likely only in private collections
2. **"U.F.O." Lacerba vinyl** (1997/1999) — UK-only release; rare import
3. **SUBurbani 1997-2004** — unauthorized but historically significant; documents Mescal dispute era
4. **Anomalia Subsonica EP** — only available with out-of-print book (Giunti, 2003)
5. **Cielo tangenziale owest DVD** — documents peak Traffic Festival era; physical release rare
6. **Il diluvio remixato** — digital-only Italian DnB remix EP; at risk of being lost if not archived
7. **Non identificato instrumentals** — original versions before UK remix; possibly only on "Preso blu" single

### Band Connections to Crawl
- **Linea 77** — direct member link (Caesar Palace)
- **Africa Unite** — Max Casacci's previous band
- **99 Posse** — Samuel video appearance
- **Bluvertigo / Morgan** — production collaboration, shared Sanremo stage

- **Pierfunk (Motel Connection, LNRipley)** — ex-member projects
- **Sikitikis, Cinemavolta** — Casasonica label discoveries

### Context for Demotape Database
Subsonica represents the **bridge between Italian underground and mainstream**. They demonstrate how the Turin club/centro sociale scene of the 1990s produced acts that could achieve commercial success without sacrificing experimental edge. Their 30-year career with the same core members is unprecedented in Italian rock.

The **Mentale Strumentale** story (lost 2004 recording released 2020) is a perfect case study for the Demotape project's mission — catalogs preserved from obscurity, artistic work rescued from label disputes.
