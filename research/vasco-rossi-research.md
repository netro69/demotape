## 1. Band History

### Overview
Vasco Rossi (born 7 February 1952 in Zocca, province of Modena, Italy) — also known mononymously as Vasco or by his nickname Il Blasco — is an Italian singer-songwriter and poet. He is one of the best-selling and most influential figures in Italian rock history, with over 30 albums (including studio, live, and compilations) and more than 250 songs written across a career spanning five decades.

### Formation & Early Years
- **Born:** 7 February 1952, Zocca (Modena), Emilia-Romagna, Italy
- **Encouraged by mother** Novella Corsi to study music as a child
- **1975:** Co-founded "Punto Radio" in Zocca — one of the first private/free radio stations in Emilia-Romagna — with friend Marco Gherardi
- **Late 1970s:** Moved to Bologna to study accountancy (ragioneria) while pursuing music in the Bologna-Emilia underground scene
- **1977:** Released first single "Jenny"/"Silvia" on Lotus label

### Genre & Style
- **Primary Genres:** Rock, Pop Rock, Cantautore (singer-songwriter)
- **Style:** Italian rock, stadium rock, provocative lyrics, poetic songwriting
- **Active Years:** 1975–present (50+ year career)

### Backing Bands
Vasco Rossi is a solo singer-songwriter, not a traditional band. He has performed with various backing bands:
- **Steve Rogers Band** (1980s) — his first backing band, formed between Modena and Bologna
- **VascoNonStop Live** (2000s–present) — current touring configuration

### Current Status
**Still active.** Vasco Rossi continues to tour and release new music. His most recent studio album is *Siamo qui* (2021). He remains one of Italy's biggest live draws.
