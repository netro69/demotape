# Research: Mauro 'Theo' Teardo (id=92)

**Status:** ✅ Complete — extensive trace found

## Identity

- **Real name:** Mauro Teardo
- **Pseudonym:** Teho Teardo (also Theo Teardo)
- **Born:** 19 September 1966, Pordenone, Friuli, Italy
- **Genres:** Dark ambient, Noise music, Musica concreta, Noise rock, Film score
- **Active:** 1986–present
- **Instruments:** Guitar, clarinet (early), sampler, electronics
- **Labels:** Old Europa Cafe, Broken Flag, Requiem Productions, Minus Habens Records, Sub/Mission Records, Helter Skelter Records, Specula (his own label, founded 2011)

## Key Career Phases

### 1. M.T.T. Records (late 1980s)
- Released solo material under pseudonym M.T.T. on compilations:
  - TV Ch 009 (Old Europa Cafe)
  - Sound From Hands (Minus Habens Records)
  - Stinky Horse Fuckers (Stinky Horse Fuck, 1993)

### 2. Meathead (late 1980s–1996)
- **Founding member** of Italian noise/industrial rock band
- Members: Tim Pantado (bass, sampler), Plastic Girl (ex-HitlerSS, Waalt Diisneey Production — drums), Valentine Dunmore (percussion, Atari sampling)
- Albums:
  - *Street Knowledge* (1992, Helter Skelter Records) — industrial rock/industrial hip-hop crossover
  - *Meathead Against The World* (1996, Sub/Mission Records)
- Paolo Favati (Pankow member) produced some tracks
- Split in 1996

### 3. Collaborations (1990s–2000s)
- **Mick Harris** (Scorn, Napalm Death) — collaborated on *Same Here* (Sub/Mission, 1996)
- **Jim Coleman** — collaborated
- **Lydia Lunch** — collaborated
- **Scott McCloud** (Girls Against Boys) — project called **Operator**
- **Erik Friedlander** (Masada, John Zorn) — *Giorni Rubati* (BiP_HOp, 2006), inspired by Pier Paolo Pasolini poetry

### 4. Film Score Composer (2000s–present)
- Composed for 13+ films including:
  - *Denti* — Gabriele Salvatores
  - *The Family Friend* — Paolo Sorrentino
  - *Slam* — Andrea Molaioli
  - *Il Divo* — Paolo Sorrentino
  - *A Quiet Life* (2010) — Toni Servillo

### 5. Teho Teardo & Blixa Bargeld (2013–present)
- Collaboration with Blixa Bargeld (Einstürzende Neubauten, Nick Cave & The Bad Seeds)
- Albums:
  - *Still Smiling* (Specula, 2013) — double album, critical acclaim
  - *Spring EP* (Specula, 2014)
  - *Fall EP* (Specula, 2017)
  - *Nerissimo* (Specula, 2017)
  - *Christian & Mauro* (Specula, 2024)
- Met at Swans concert in Berlin, 1988

### 6. Recent Work (2024–2026)
- *Ellipses dans l'harmonie — Lumi nel buio* (Specula, 2025)
- *Teho Teardo plays Twin Peaks and Other Infinitives* (Specula, Oct 2025)
- *Viaggio al termine della notte* — theatrical piece with Elio Germano (Céline adaptation)
- Radio show "Oltre Il Confine" on Radio Rai
