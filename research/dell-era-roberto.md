## Biography

Roberto Dell'Era (born 1968, Italy), also known by the pseudonym **Rob Winston**, is an Italian singer-songwriter and multi-instrumentalist active since 1980. He first came to wider public attention in **2006** when Manuel Agnelli chose him to replace Andrea Viti as bassist for the iconic Italian alternative rock band **Afterhours**. He has been a core member of the band since 2005/2006, contributing to albums including *Germi* (2025).

Dell'Era is described as an "Englishman by adoption" — he spent over 10 years breathing the English and international music scene before returning to Italy. He is a true multi-instrumentalist: bass, guitar, keyboards, vocals, and more. His stage name "Rob Winston" was adopted for his psychedelic side project *The Winstons*.

A Lost Highways interview described him as "the one who argues most" within Afterhours, hinting at his strong creative personality. He's known for penchant for vintage Italian-made guitars (particularly his 12-string REX).

## Key Collaborations & Side Projects

### 1. Afterhours (2005/2006–present)
- Role: Bassist, keyboardist, vocalist
- Bandmates: Manuel Agnelli (vocals/guitar), Giorgio Prette, Rodrigo D'Erasmo, Giorgio Ciccarelli
- Formed 1985 in Milan; named after Velvet Underground song
- One of the most important Italian alternative rock bands

### 2. The Winstons (2015/2016–present)
- A psychedelic/progressive rock trio
- Members:
  - **Lino Gitto** (a.k.a. Linnon Winston) — vocals, organ, drums
  - **Roberto Dell'Era** (a.k.a. Rob Winston) — vocals, bass
  - **Enrico Gabrielli** (a.k.a. Enro Winston) — organ, sax, bass clarinet, electric piano
- Guest musician: Roberto D'Azzan (trumpet)
- Sound: "keyboard/bass/drum" power trio bridging jazz, rock, and psychedelia — compared to early Soft Machine
- Releases on their own Bandcamp

## Summary

Roberto Dell'Era is a key figure in the Italian indie/alternative scene — a multi-instrumentalist who bridges the iconic **Afterhours** with psychedelic side-project **The Winstons**, plus collaborations with cinematic funk (Calibro 35), indie singer-songwriters (Dente), and various supergroup projects. His solo career spans three albums (2012–2018), and he remains active in 2025 with Afterhours' latest release *Germi*. His pseudonymous identity "Rob Winston" reflects a more experimental, psychedelic side distinct from his Afterhours work.
