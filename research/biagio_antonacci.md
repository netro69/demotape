## Overview

Biagio Antonacci is a mainstream Italian pop/rock singer-songwriter from Milan. He is NOT an underground noise rock artist but rather one of Italy's most commercially successful modern pop singers (over 10 million records sold). He is included in the Demotape database because of his collaborative connections to the Florentine underground scene via **Saverio Lanza** (RockGalileo), who served as guitarist, bassist, pianist, and songwriter for Antonacci from 1996 onwards.
