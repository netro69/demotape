## Overview

**eMi** is the solo stage name of **Emilia Nebl**, an Italian singer-songwriter and music producer from **Cles, Trentino**, Italy. She is also **1/4 of the band Dablio** (@d4blioband). Nebl studied Music Production and Vocal Performance at **Point Blank Music School** in London and currently splits her time between London and Italy. In addition to her music career, she works as a graphic designer.
