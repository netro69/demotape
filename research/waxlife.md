## Overview

**Waxlife** is the primary professional moniker of **Simone Lanza**, an Italian DJ, music producer, sound designer, and composer from **Malnate** (Varese province), **Italy**. Active for over 20 years, he is an eclectic figure in the Italian and international electronic music scene, known for his work in deep house, nu-disco, downtempo, and dance-punk. He has performed in 15+ countries and released music on labels including **Ghost Records**, **Trend Records**, **Museek Records**, and **Disco Volante Recordings**.

> ⚠️ **Note:** Despite being entered in the demotape database as "Band ID 110," Waxlife is a **solo electronic music project**, not a punk/hardcore band. The demotape database appears to catalog a wide range of artists beyond just punk/hardcore demo tapes.

## History & Origin

- **Origin:** Malnate, Varese, Lombardy, Italy
- **Based in:** Varese, Italy
- **Active:** ~2004–present (20+ years)
- **Name chosen:** 2011 (per Facebook post: "Ho scelto questo nome nel 2011")
- **Background:** Simone Lanza began as a DJ and gradually expanded into production, sound design, and composition. He has worked on music for films, advertisements, TV shows, and fashion brands including **Moschino, Max Mara, Valentino, and Diesel**.
- **Studio:** Works with **Jackleg Studio** (sound design/production studio).

## Interesting Facts

1. 🎧 **20+ year DJ career:** Simone Lanza has performed in 15+ countries and played at major Italian events/festivals including Siouxie (Venezia), Lumen Festival (Vicenza), Hyped Music Festival & Elita (Milano), Eleva (Reggio Emilia), Wave of Sound, Albizzate Valley Festival, Novara Jazz, and Astoria.
2. 👔 **Fashion world crossover:** He has composed music for major fashion brands including Moschino, Max Mara, Valentino, and Diesel.
3. 🎬 **Film/TV composer:** Worked as sound designer, mixer, and composer on films, advertisements, and TV shows.
4. 📼 **Archival project:** In 2025, he released previously unreleased beats from 2009–2015 as the *New Adventures In Lo-Fi* EP series, dubbed onto DIY cassette tapes.
5. 🏠 **Hometown roots:** From Malnate, a small town in Varese province, Lombardy — part of the northern Italian electronic music scene centered around Varese/Milan.
6. 🔄 **Name origin:** Chose the name "Waxlife" in 2011; in 2021 he announced the project was "retiring" but continues to release under the name as a "second name."
7. 🎵 **"Clocks" breakthrough:** His track "Clocks" (Strictly Groove Recordings) entered the top 50 on Juno Download's nu-disco chart within a week and was selected by Beatport and iTunes Canada as a featured deep-house release.

## Summary

Waxlife (Simone Lanza) is an Italian electronic music producer and DJ from Malnate/Varese, active for over 20 years. His music spans deep house, nu-disco, downtempo, and dance-punk. He has released two albums (*Cecilia* in 2014, *Patterns* in 2018) on Ghost Records, plus numerous EPs and singles. He is also known for his Waxlife Disco Service alias (Trend Records), archival lo-fi beat releases (Museek Records), and remixes for artists like To You Mom:, Be a Bear, and da Black Jezus. Beyond music, he works as a sound designer for fashion brands (Moschino, Max Mara, Valentino, Diesel) and film/TV. He is not a punk/hardcore band but a solo electronic music project.
