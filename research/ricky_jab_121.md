## Career Summary

Ricky Jabarin rose to prominence on TikTok during COVID-19 (March 2020) by creating pop-punk and metal remixes from other users' TikTok videos. He sang in collegiate a cappella groups at Penn State before pivoting to professional music during the pandemic.

He expanded into production and engineering, building a reputation for radio-ready pop-punk and metal guitar work. In 2022-2023, he formed **Unsafe, Unsound** with Tyler Morris (the NYC-based producer known as Westend, not to be confused with the vocalist from Tired Violence who shares the same name). The duo runs a TikTok account @unsafeunsound (67K+ followers, 623K+ likes) and competed in the Ernie Ball Battle of the Bands for Warped Tour 2025.

He currently tours as lead guitarist for **Taylor Acorn** (2026 tour dates via Live Nation). He also operates as a session musician/producer for hire, offering guitar, mixing, and vocal tuning services.

## Research Notes

- **No Italian underground connection.** This artist is entirely American — Philly-based, Penn State-educated, touring nationally with Taylor Acorn.
- **TikTok origin story:** Like Mathieux Davis, Ricky Jab's career was catalyzed by COVID-19 lockdowns and social media. He leveraged viral content to build a music career without traditional industry gatekeeping.
- **Name confusion risk:** "Ricky Jab" is sometimes stylized as "RickyJab" (Apple Music, Spotify). His full name "Ricky Jabarin" appears on LinkedIn, Genius credits, and production work.
