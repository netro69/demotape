# Yoad Nevo

## Overview

**Yoad Nevo** is an Israeli-born, London-based multi-platinum music producer, mix engineer, keyboardist, and audio technologist. Over a 25+ year career, he has worked with a remarkably diverse roster of artists spanning pop, rock, electronic, and alternative genres. In addition to his production and mixing work, Nevo is the owner of **Nevo Sound Studios** in Central London and serves as Product Manager and co-inventor of professional audio plugins for **Waves Audio**, contributing to over 100 software product releases.

### Career Background

Nevo began playing guitar at age 10 and exploring electronic instruments in his youth. By 17, he was working in professional recording studios, developing expertise in digital audio production. His career accelerated through the late 1990s and 2000s as he became an in-demand mixer for major UK pop and electronic acts.

In 2006, he built **Nevo Sound Studios** in Central London, a hybrid facility that merges analog warmth (centered around a 60-channel vintage Neve V51 mixing desk) with cutting-edge digital and immersive monitoring. The studio is recognized as one of London's leading large-scale production facilities.

Nevo is also deeply embedded in audio technology development. As a Waves Audio product manager and co-inventor, he helped design some of the industry's most widely used plugins, including the **NLS Non-Linear Summer**, **V-Comp**, **L3 Multimaximizer**, and numerous other tools that shaped modern digital audio workflows. His contributions to Waves Audio have earned both a **Technical Grammy Award** and an **Engineering Emmy Award** for audio product development.

## Interesting Facts & Significance

1. **Dual Expertise**: Nevo is one of the rare figures who operates at the highest level both as a creative music professional (producer/mixer for Grammy-winning artists) and as a technical audio engineer/designer (co-inventor of industry-standard plugins).

2. **Award Recognition**: His Waves Audio product work has earned both a **Technical Grammy Award** and an **Engineering Emmy Award**, placing him among the most decorated audio technologists in the industry.

3. **Plugin Pioneer**: Nevo contributed to the "digital revolution in music production" through early Waves Audio plugin development. Tools like NLS Non-Linear Summer are still considered industry standards for analog console emulation.

4. **Nevo Sound Studios**: His Central London studio features a rare **60-channel vintage Neve V51** mixing desk and supports Dolby Atmos immersive mixing, making it one of the most technically advanced facilities in the UK.

5. **Production Style**: Known for "hard-hitting pop" mixes with clarity, punch, and spacious stereo imaging — a signature approach that made him a go-to mixer for UK pop acts in the 2000s and beyond.

6. **Educational Outreach**: Nevo maintains an active YouTube channel with tutorials, mixing walkthroughs, and Waves plugin demonstrations, sharing professional techniques with aspiring producers.

7. **Early Career**: Started in professional studios at just 17 years old, building a career from the ground up during the transition from analog to digital production.

8. **Chart Dominance**: With 30+ number-one singles and 50+ top-10 albums, Nevo is among the most commercially successful mix engineers in UK pop history.
