## Career Summary

David Michael Frank is a Washington State-born artist who fronted the electronic pop duo **Future Sunsets** from 2015-2022. After 10+ years in bands, he launched his solo career in late 2022. He relocated from Seattle to Los Angeles to Nashville, where he now operates **DMF Studios** — a recording and creative space.

He has a massive social media presence: 2.1M TikTok followers (31.7M+ likes), 920K+ YouTube views, 127K+ Instagram followers. Known for viral original songs, inventive covers, high-range vocals, and cinematic pop-rock production.

In 2024, he released the EP "Running For Life" — described as "a captivating blend of punk rock, alternative pop, and rock with extraordinary high vocals" (TJ Malana review).
