# The Smokers

**The Smokers** is a Spanish Hard Rock band formed in March 2000 in Estepona, Málaga (Andalusia), Spain. Known for their classic hard rock sound inspired by 70s-80s rock 'n' roll, the band has released multiple albums and built a strong reputation in the Spanish rock scene over two decades.

## History

### Formation & Early Years (2000–2001)

The band was born in March 2000 from an idea long harbored by the guitarists as a casual hobby. Everything changed in September 2000 when they won the 1st Edition of the Mancurock Festival in Estepona, which convinced them to take the project seriously. They recorded their first demo in their rehearsal space and entered the 15th Edition of the Festival de Música Joven de Málaga (also called Eduardo Ocón), where they reached the finals and won the jury's first prize. This led them to the semifinals of the Festival Provincial de Música (San Pepe Rock / Proyecta 2001).

### Second Demo & Early Recognition (2001–2002)

The band recorded their second demo, "Let me see," at Musisol studio, which allowed them to play venues along the Costa del Sol. In November 2001, they entered **Dune Studios** with producer **Sergio Cascales** (a respected Malaga producer with an extensive resume), who helped them find the sound they had been seeking and became known as "the fifth Smoker."

This demo brought further recognition: they were finalists in the prestigious Villa de Madrid 02 and won the Móstoles Rock contest, which earned them a slot at **Festimad 02** alongside acts like **Rammstein**, **Jamiroquai**, and **Säber**. They also received praise from media outlets including Carlos Pina of Radio 3, Popular 1 magazine, and Heavy Rock.

In September 2002, they returned to the studio for a third demo with Sergio Cascales, aiming to attract Madrid labels. During a Madrid trip, they visited **Nano Ruiz**, owner of **Lengua Armada** and did their first radio interview with Carlos Pina on "Bienvenidos al Paraíso."

### Crisis & Debut Album (2003–2004)

2003 was their worst year: almost no concerts (except Estepona Rock 03) and no label interest. On the verge of breaking up, they decided to record an album independently. They spent a year (balancing day jobs with studio time) recording their debut at Dune Studios with Sergio Cascales.

After approaching various figures (including Jorge Escobedo of Säber), **Nano Ruiz** of **Lengua Armada** finally signed them. Their debut album, **"Giving Up,"** was released in October 2004 to widespread critical acclaim. They toured extensively and opened for major acts including **Scorpions**, **L.A. Guns**, and **Adler's Appetite**. They were also finalists in the Villa de Bilbao 05 European competition.

### Second Album & Continued Growth (2007–2009)

In 2007, they recorded **"Still Giving"** at Dune Studios with Sergio Cascales. Though the music industry was changing and the album didn't sell as well as the debut, it earned unanimous critical praise across media including bloggers, IndyRock, Popular 1, Heavy Rock, and Kerrang.

### Third Album: Spanish-Language Shift (2009–2012)

In 2009, they recorded **"Dirección Sur"** at Estudios La Sala de Máquinas in Lorca (Murcia), again produced by Sergio Cascales with engineer Manuel Torroglosa. This marked a shift: it was their first album with Spanish lyrics, released in 2011/2012.

### Later Albums (2015–2019)

- **"Roads Less Traveled"** (2015) – 9 tracks, blues rock/funk influences, self-released
- **"Still Living"** (2018/2019) – Album presented in concert in Madrid on November 16, 2018, at Sala Ritmo & Compás

## Musical Style

The Smokers' sound is rooted in classic Hard Rock and Rock 'N' Roll from the 1970s-1980s, with strong vocal harmonies and choirs sometimes influenced by Black Music traditions. Their music features powerful guitar riffs, driving percussion, and heartfelt vocals. Early albums were primarily in English; with "Dirección Sur" they transitioned to Spanish lyrics while maintaining their hard rock foundation. "Roads Less Traveled" showed blues rock and funk influences.

## Connections & Associated Acts

### Label & Production Network
- **Lengua Armada (Lenguaje Constructivo)** – Their long-time label, founded by **Nano Ruiz** in 2000. Nano was previously guitarist for **Habeas Corpus** and **Deniro**. The label also released albums by **Estirpe**, **RIP KC**, and others.
- **Sergio Cascales** – Key producer who shaped their sound at Dune Studios. Often called "el quinto Smoker."

- **L.A. Guns** – Opening act

The Tragic Company has also collaborated on the track "Out Of My Way." Members: Juanma Medina (vocals/guitar), Mariano Alcobendas (guitar), Alan Voreaux (bass), Jose Luis Fernández (drums).

## Legacy & Notable Facts

- The band's name and aesthetic play on rock 'n' roll rebellion and classic rock imagery.
- José Antonio Seoane's death in August 2024 was widely mourned in the Spanish rock community. He was a respected firefighter and dedicated musician who balanced his day job with two decades in the band.
- Their 2004 debut "Giving Up" is considered a standout of the Spanish hard rock scene of that era.
- The shift to Spanish lyrics with "Dirección Sur" was seen as a bold creative move that paid off critically.
- They are one of the longest-running hard rock bands from the Málaga/Andalusia region.
- Their consistent collaboration with producer Sergio Cascales across all major albums gave them a cohesive, recognizable sound.
