## Overview

**Asher Swissa** (Hebrew: אשר סוויסה) is an Israeli electronic music producer, DJ, and multi-project artist, best known as the founding member of the legendary Israeli psytrance act **Skazi**. Born on **July 8, 1975**, in **Jerusalem, Israel**, Swissa has been active in electronic music since 1998 and is considered one of the foundational figures of the Israeli psytrance scene. He operates under two primary identities: **Skazi** (his harder, psychedelic trance/electronic rock sound) and **ASHER SWISSA** (his side project exploring melodic techno and progressive house). He is also the owner/operator of the **Chemical Crew** record label, a long-running imprint in the psytrance world.

---

## Genre

Swissa's musical output spans multiple electronic genres across his career:

- **Psytrance / Full-On Psytrance** — His primary and most recognized genre under the Skazi moniker. Early work fused live electric guitar riffs with hardcore techno drums and psychedelic textures.
- **Progressive Psytrance** — Later Skazi albums and ASHER SWISSA releases lean into more progressive, melodic structures.
- **Melodic Techno / Progressive House** — His ASHER SWISSA side project (active since ~2019–2020) explores softer tempos, vocal-driven tracks, and club-friendly melodic techno.
- **Electro-Punk / Trance-Rock** — Early Skazi material (2000–2004) blended punk rock energy with trance and hard house elements, sometimes referred to as "handbag emocore" by critics.
- **Dubstep / Industrial** — Experimental releases incorporate heavier bass and industrial textures.
- **Hard House / Techno** — Chemical Crew label releases often share characteristics with these styles.

## History

### Early Years & Punk Roots (1990–1998)
Swissa began his musical career as a **punk rocker** in 1990, forming the group **Sartan Hashad** (Hebrew for "Breast Cancer") with three friends. The band played small venues in **Jerusalem** and **Tel Aviv**, achieving limited but notable success within the tight-knit Israeli punk scene. He remained with Sartan Hashad for approximately seven years (1990–1997), then spent time with **Rock Ba-Yarok** before shifting his focus to electronic music.

### Formation of Skazi (1998)
In 1998, Swissa partnered with **Assaf Bivas** (DJ B-Bass) to form **Skazi**, a project designed to blend Swissa's punk/rock sensibilities with Bivas's experience as a club DJ (Bivas had previously run the project **Mental Orange**). The result was a unique fusion of **psytrance, electro-punk, and hard house** — a sound that stood out in the late-1990s Israeli electronic scene.

### Breakthrough & Chemical Crew Label
Skazi's debut album, **Animal** (September 2000), became a landmark release in Israeli psytrance. The album combined **live electric guitar riffs** with **hardcore techno drums** and psychedelic production — a signature that defined the early Skazi sound. Swissa founded the **Chemical Crew** record label, which became a hub for psytrance, hard house, and techno releases, not only for his own projects but also for collaborations with major artists.

### Collaborations & Label Work
Through Chemical Crew, Swissa worked with numerous high-profile psytrance and electronic artists, including:
- Infected Mushroom
- Astrix
- GMS
- Talamasca
- Eskimo
- Void
- Exaile
- Psychotic Micro

### Solo Evolution (2010s–Present)
After Assaf Bivas's departure, Skazi became Swissa's solo project. Albums like **My Way** (2012) and **PsyScription** (2015) showed a more refined, diverse production style. By the late 2010s, Swissa began splitting his output between **Skazi** (harder, festival-oriented psytrance) and **ASHER SWISSA** (melodic, vocal-driven progressive house/melodic techno).

### "The Secret Project" & Mainstream Crossover (2020–2024)
In 2020–2021, ASHER SWISSA became known as "The Secret Project" — a deliberate shift toward melodic techno and progressive house. This era produced some of Swissa's biggest club hits, including "Daleko," "Where Are You Going," "Enta Omri (Remix)," and "Buzzing" (2022). The **Buzzing Tour** (2021–2022) saw over 150 shows worldwide. By 2023–2024, Swissa was collaborating with major names like **Korolova**, **Miss Monique**, and **Like Mike**, and releasing on labels including **BlackHole Recordings**, **Siona Records**, **Reborn Records**, and **Helicon**.

### Controversy (2023–2025)
Swissa has been vocal in his support of Israeli troops during the Gaza conflict. In November 2023, he participated in a video by **The Civil Front Movement** (Hachazit Haezrahit) that promised to "dance on the ruins of your houses in Gaza." He has performed for IDF soldiers before they entered Gaza and at the **Re'im music festival massacre** memorial (November 28, 2023). In July 2024, he dedicated a **Tomorrowland** set to Israeli troops. In July 2025, his return to Tomorrowland was **cancelled** due to security concerns and political controversy, despite having performed there for 13 consecutive years. Swissa responded on Instagram stating "my mission is to bring unity" and calling to "make music not war."
