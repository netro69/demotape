## Overview

**One Dimensional Man** was an Italian **noise rock / post-hardcore** band from **Venice (Venezia), Italy**, active from **1996 to 2021** (with extended hiatuses 2006-2010 and 2011-2016). Their sound fused noise rock and post-hardcore with blues influences. The band's name references Herbert Marcuse's 1964 critical theory book *One-Dimensional Man: Studies in the Ideology of Advanced Industrial Society*.

> **Note:** Despite being in the demotape database, no demoscene connection has been found. This band is purely noise rock/post-hardcore.

## History & Origin

- **Origin:** Venice (Venezia), Veneto, Italy
- **Formed:** 1996
- **Active periods:** 1996–2006, 2010–2011, 2016–2021
- **Scenes:** Italian noise rock / post-hardcore / alternative underground

### Formation
Born in 1996 from the idea of Pierpaolo Capovilla (vocals/bass) and Massimo Sartor (guitar). Initially used a drum machine before recruiting Dario Perissutti (guitarist from the Venice indie scene). Ugo Mazzia (manager of Fluxus) assisted the band in some circumstances and relationships.

### Key Evolution
- **1997:** Debut album on Wide Records (Pisa), critical acclaim
- **1998:** Massimo Sartor left; replaced by Giulio Favero → sound shifted toward blues and 80s rock (Birthday Party, The Cramps, Scratch Acid, Butthole Surfers)
- **2000:** Second album *1000 Doses of Love* — concept album about love/failure
- **2001:** Third album *You Kill Me* on Gamma Pop; first music video (MTV, Rock TV, etc.)
- **2003:** First European tour (Berlin, Vienna, Lugano, Brussels, Hamburg); Giulio Favero left (became producer); Carlo Veneziano joined
- **2004:** Fourth album *Take Me Away* on Ghost Records / Midfinger Records
- **2005:** Dario Perissutti left; Francesco Valente joined; Capovilla formed Il Teatro degli Orrori
- **2010:** Reunion tour with Giulio Favero + Luca Bottigliero (Mesmerico); released *The Box* (4-CD box set)
- **2011:** Fifth album *A Better Man* on La Tempesta Dischi with guests: Eugene Robinson (Oxbow), Franz Stahl (Scream), Bob Rifo (Bloody Beetroots), Enrico Gabrielli (Calibro 35), Rodrigo D'Erasmo (Afterhours), Jacopo Battaglia (Zu), Aucan
- **2016-2018:** Reunited with Franz Valente (drums) + Carlo Veneziano (guitar); recorded *You Don't Exist*
- **2018:** Sixth album *You Don't Exist* on La Tempesta International / Goodfellas; cover art by Michele Bubacco (Venetian artist)
- **2021:** Band concluded activities

## Genre

- **Noise Rock** (primary)
- **Post-Hardcore**
- **Blues** influences
- **Alternative Rock** / Indie Rock
- **Garage Rock** elements (later period)

Sound described as raw, angry, textured — heavy distorted guitars, visceral vocals, propulsive rhythms with bluesy undertones. Considered one of the best exponents of Italian hardcore-noise in the 1990s alongside Uzeda, UltraviXen, and Fluxus.

**Influences cited:** Birthday Party, The Cramps, Scratch Acid, Butthole Surfers, The Jesus Lizard, No Means No, Nirvana, Sonic Youth, Shellac, Steve Albini sound.

## Key Events & Achievements

1. **Tora Tora Festival:** Participated multiple times (2001, 2003, 2004, 2005) alongside Afterhours, Marlene Kuntz, La Crus, Verdena, Meganoidi, Modena City Ramblers, Africa Unite, etc. The festival (ideated by Manuel Agnelli/Afterhours and Mescal) was a landmark touring event for Italian alternative music.

2. **Extensive touring:** 1997-1998 alone: 100+ concerts across Italy, Croatia, Slovenia, Czech Republic, Slovakia. Opened for Blonde Redhead, The Cows, Kepone, Fluxus, Uzeda, dEUS, Jon Spencer Blues Explosion, Therapy?, Demolition Doll Rods, Delta 72, Melvins, Motörhead (Cagliari).

3. **Label evolution:** Wide Records (Pisa) → Gamma Pop → Ghost Records (Varese) / Midfinger Records (Varese) → La Tempesta Dischi (Pordenone)

4. **Hiatus and rebirth:** Two major hiatuses (2006-2010, 2011-2016). After 2004's *Take Me Away*, Capovilla focused on Il Teatro degli Orrori before returning to ODM.

5. **Music videos:** "You Kill Me" (2001, dir. G. Cecinelli/Digital Desk Roma) and "Tell Me Marie" (2004, dir. Mauro Lovisetto, won MEI Faenza 2004 best indie video).

6. **MTV Supersonic:** Performed three live tracks on the TV show.

7. **Ghost Day:** Performed at this Varese label's festival with Zu, Karate, Ex.

8. **Neapolis Festival:** Performed with AIR, David Byrne, Arab Strap.
