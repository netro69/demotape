## Career Summary

Jaclyn Glenn is a Los Angeles-based YouTuber and singer-songwriter. She rose to prominence through her YouTube channel (826K+ subscribers) covering topics including religious commentary, atheism, social activism, relationships, and vlogs. She also performs music — both original songs and covers — distributed through YouTube, Spotify, and other streaming platforms.

She is engaged to David Michael Frank (DMF, Band 116), an American pop/alternative artist formerly of Future Sunsets. The couple collaborates musically and has appeared together in multiple music videos and social media content.
