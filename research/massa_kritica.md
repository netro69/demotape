# Massakritica — Research Notes

**Band ID:** 12  
**Location:** Varese, Italy  
**Genres:** Ska punk, rock, hip hop, reggae  
**Years active:** 2002–present (evolved from Amplessi Komplessi, 1993–2002)

---

## History

Massakritica formed in **2002** in Varese, Italy, from the ashes of **Amplessi Komplessi**, a well-known Italian ska band that had been active since **1993**. Amplessi Komplessi played a style of ska heavily contaminated with rock, hip hop, and reggae sounds and released the album *"Fuori dalle skatole!"*.

The transition from Amplessi Komplessi to Massakritica marked a continuation of the Varese ska/punk scene with a refreshed lineup and sound.

## Musical Style

Massakritica's sound is rooted in **ska punk** but heavily blended with:
- **Rock** — driving guitar riffs and energetic rhythms
- **Hip hop** — vocal delivery and beat influences
- **Reggae** — dub and offbeat rhythms

This fusion places them firmly in the Italian ska-core / punk scene that flourished in the late 1990s and early 2000s.
