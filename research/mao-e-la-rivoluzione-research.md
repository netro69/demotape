## 4. Key Events & Milestones

- **1993:** Band formed in Turin
- **1996:** Debut album *Sale* released on Virgin Records
- **1997:** Second album *Casa* released; band name shortened to "Mao" for Sanremo Giovani participation with "Romantico"
- **1997:** Opened for Oasis (major international exposure)
- **1997–present:** "Il Salotto di Mao" — weekly live music event at LAB Torino, Piazza Vittorio Veneto; 1000+ artists hosted
- **1998:** Band effectively disbanded; Mauro Gurlino continues as solo artist
- **2001:** Solo debut *Black Mokette* released (Sony Music, produced by Morgan)
- **2011:** *Meglio tardi che Mao* released
- **2019–2020:** Return with singles "Nudi alla meta," "Le cose," "Scusa caro vicino"

## 5. Musical Style & Influences

### Genre & Sound
- **Primary:** Alternative rock, pop rock, electronic rock
- **Elements:** Psychedelic rock, funk, electronic music, cantautore (singer-songwriter)
- **Style:** Eclectic fusion of rock with psychedelic, funk, and electronic textures; experimental pop sensibility

### Influences
- **The Doors** (psychedelic rock influence, cited in interviews)
- **Funk and electronic music** (integrated into rock framework)
- **Italian cantautore tradition** (singer-songwriter craft)
- **1990s British rock** (Oasis, Britpop era — reflected in their Virgin Records signing and Oasis support slot)

### Critical Reception
- Described as "enfant prodige" of the Turin music scene
- "Casa" (1997) characterized as "pop rock italiano con tocchi psichedelici, funk ed elettronica" (Italian pop rock with psychedelic, funk, and electronic touches)
- Considered a bridge between Turin underground and mainstream Italian rock

## 9. Cultural Context & Demotape Relevance

### Turin (Torino) — The Creative Crucible
Mao e la Rivoluzione emerged from Turin's vibrant 1990s music scene, which also produced Subsonica, Africa Unite, Linea 77, and Mau Mau. Key factors:
- **Post-industrial transformation:** FIAT factory closures → creative spaces
- **Piazza Vittorio:** Cultural hub; where "Il Salotto di Mao" takes place
- **Centro Sociale circuit:** Alternative music infrastructure
- **MTV Italy:** Mao co-hosted programs, bridging underground and mainstream

### Underground-to-Mainstream Bridge
- **1993:** Born in Turin clubs and underground venues
- **1996:** Signed to Virgin Records (major label) — rare for Italian alternative acts
- **1997:** Sanremo Giovani participation — underground enters mainstream
- **1997:** Opened for Oasis — international credibility
- Maintained artistic integrity while achieving commercial success

### "Il Salotto di Mao" (1997–present)
- Weekly live music event at LAB Torino, Piazza Vittorio Veneto
- Running for 25+ years
- Hosted 1000+ artists
- Produced by CortoCorto (Mao's studio)
- Represents Mao's ongoing commitment to nurturing Turin's music scene
