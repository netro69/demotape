# To You Mom — Research Notes

> Band ID: 30 | Last updated: 2026-09-17

## Overview

**To You Mom** (stylized as **To You Mom:** with trailing colon) is an Italian **electro pop / darkwave-inflected synthpop** duo based in **Milano/Trento, Italy**. Their sound is described as "hypnotic and moody," built around "gorgeous melodic inflections and ethereal soundscapes."

## History & Origin

- **Formed:** 2011 in Italy
- **Origin story:** The duo began from a casual dinner between Luca Lorenzi and **Marco Ricci** of **Casa del Mirto**. What started as a one-off musical experiment evolved into a long-term project.
- **Founding concept:** Their first EP, *I Am Ian*, was conceived as a one-off project celebrating the stormy, extraordinary life of **imaginary astronaut Ian Coleman**. The duo wasn't originally intended to be a permanent project.
- **Cartography connection:** Luca Lorenzi and Massimiliano Santoni met at **Italy's International Conference on Cartography and GIS Mapping**. Their shared interest in maps and space became a creative foundation for the project.
- **Name trivia:** The name "To You Mom:" includes a trailing colon, which appears consistently across all official releases and platforms.

## Interesting Facts

1. 🗺️ **Cartography origin:** The duo met at an academic GIS/cartography conference — their shared love of maps and space shaped the project's identity.
2. ✋ **Sign language video:** The "Charming Karma" music video focused on **Italian Sign Language (LIS)**, starring Ilaria Galbusera and Alessio Convito — a deliberate choice highlighting that sign languages are not universal.
3. 🏠 **Quarantine acoustic:** During the 2020 COVID-19 lockdown, the duo recorded a home acoustic version of "Blow," documented by Italian outlet *l'Adige.it*.
4. 👨‍🚀 **Fictional astronaut:** Their debut EP *I Am Ian* is a concept piece about the imaginary astronaut Ian Coleman — a deliberately "dirty," raw work.
5. 🌐 **Active after 15+ years:** From a 2011 dinner-party joke to releasing singles in 2026, the duo has maintained creative output for over 14 years.
6. 🎹 **Multi-instrumentalists:** Both members play multiple instruments — Lorenzi (vocals/keys) and Santoni (guitar/keys/pads) — contributing to their layered sound.
7. 📍 **Geographic spread:** Members based in two different Italian cities — Milan and Trento — reflecting their origins in different parts of northern Italy.
