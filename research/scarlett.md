# Scarlett (id=135)

## Summary

**Scarlett** is the alias of **Sara Jeanette Sangfelt**, a Swedish singer, songwriter, and topliner based in Los Angeles. She is a professional vocalist with over 200 million streams, a Gold IMPALA Award recipient, and major label releases (Sony, Universal, Warner, Spinnin', Dharma, Ultra). She is featured as the vocalist on SRNDE's 2025 single "Up To The Top" alongside producers Titanz and Martin Noiserz, which is the connection recorded in the Demotape database.

> **Identity Caveat:** There are TWO distinct artists using "Scarlett" in the EDM space — (1) Sara Sangfelt (the vocalist, aka the Demotape ID=135) and (2) an Italian DJ/producer (founder of PLSR label, @scarlettmusic_ on Instagram, 31K followers). They are NOT the same person. The Demotape connection to SRNDE confirms ID=135 is **Sara Sangfelt**.

## Notes

- **Professional profile:** Sara Sangfelt is an established session/topliner in the electronic music world with over 200M+ streams and a Gold IMPALA Award. She works independently with major labels worldwide.
- **DB anomaly:** The existing `scarlett.md` research file in this project incorrectly identifies ID=135 as the British 1990s pop duo **Scarlet** (Cheryl Parker & Jo Youle). This is wrong — the database connection to SRNDE's "Up To The Top" (2025) confirms ID=135 is the EDM vocalist **Sara Sangfelt** (aka Scarlett), not the 1990s British duo. The British duo has no connection to SRNDE or modern EDM.
- **Common confusion:** The Italian DJ/producer SCARLETT (@scarlettmusic_, PLSR label, 31K Instagram) is a DIFFERENT artist. Sara Sangfelt uses "Scarlett" as an alias primarily on NCS releases and EDM features, not as a DJ/live act.
- **Confidence:** HIGH — Apple Music credits on "Up To The Top" explicitly list "Sara Jeanette Sangfelt" as composer, confirming her identity.
- **Location:** Originally from Sweden, currently based in Los Angeles, CA. Time zone: PST.
- **Booking/management:** steven@globalistla.com (via Instagram bio). SoundBetter profile available for topline/vocal work.
