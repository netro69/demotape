## Overview

Gianni Peri is an Italian **mastering engineer, music producer, songwriter, video editor, and occasional performing artist** from Lucca, Tuscany, Italy. Active since 1999, he has worked across the electronic music spectrum — from Italian house and dance productions in the early 2000s to mastering modern darkwave, minimal synth, EBM, synthpop, and industrial releases for international artists. He is credited on 40+ releases on Discogs and has 30+ songs listed on Genius.
