## Band Overview

**Future Sunsets** is an American **pop / electronic pop / alternative pop** duo from **Los Angeles, California**. They are primarily known for posting inventive covers and original songs on YouTube, and have accumulated ~40K monthly listeners on Spotify.

### Members

| Name | Role | Born | Notes |
|------|------|------|-------|
| **David Michael Frank** | Lead vocals, multi-instrumentalist, co-founder | July 13, 1990 | Also known as DMF; runs solo/side projects under "DMF Archives" |
| **Jose Mostajo** | Guitarist, co-founder | June 27, 1990 | From Lima, Peru; previously studied guitar from age 13 |

### Origin Story

Future Sunsets formed after the disbandment of **King The Kid** (2012–2014), a pop/rock trio consisting of David Michael Frank, Jose Mostajo, and Ricky Ficarelli. When King The Kid ended in 2014, Frank and Mostajo continued as the duo Future Sunsets. They used funds from a **2014 acoustic tour** to relocate to Los Angeles.

> *"American pop/rock band formed in 2012 by Jose Mostajo, Ricky Ficarelli and David Michael Frank. Disbanded as 'King The Kid' in 2014, Jose Mostajo and David Michael Frank continued as Future Sunsets."* — Discogs

### Genre & Style

- Pop
- Electronic Pop
- Alternative Pop
- Known for both **original songs** and **inventive covers** (often acoustic or rearranged)
- Active YouTube/TikTok presence with creative videos
- Described as "dominating the YouTube & mainstream music landscape with unique original songs, inventive covers and creative videos" (Boomplay)

## Summary for Demotape Database

Future Sunsets is a **Los Angeles-based pop/electronic duo** (David Michael Frank + Jose Mostajo) with roots in the 2012–2014 pop/rock trio **King The Kid**. They are **YouTube/TikTok-era musicians** who built an audience through covers and original content, with ~40K monthly Spotify listeners. They have released numerous singles, a few EPs, and one compilation album (DMF Archives: Originals Vol. 2, 2022). They are **unsigned/independent** but distribute through DistroKid and have music on all major platforms including anime soundtrack placements (Tokyo Ghoul:re). Their most frequent collaborator is **Jaclyn Glenn**.
