## Biography

Piero Pelù (born Pietro Pelù, 10 February 1962, Florence) is an Italian singer-songwriter and rock musician, best known as the charismatic lead vocalist and co-founder of **Litfiba**, one of Italy's most influential and commercially successful rock bands. With over 20 studio albums and 7 live albums selling more than 7 million copies, Pelù is a defining figure in Italian rock.

### Early Years (1962–1980)

- Born and raised in Florence, Tuscany. As a teenager he fronted a high school band called **Mugnions** (named after the Mugnone river that flows through Fiesole into Florence). He adopted the nickname **"Pierotten"** in homage to Johnny Rotten (Sex Pistols/PiL), reflecting the punk ethos ("rotten" = marcio, considered a compliment in punk circles).
- Studied at Liceo Classico Michelangelo in Florence, graduated 1980.
- Traveled to London in 1980 intending to settle permanently, inspired by British punk. Disillusioned by what he saw as the bourgeoisification of the punk movement, he returned to Florence.

### Litfiba Era (1980–1999)

- Co-founded **Litfiba** in Florence in 1980 with Ghigo Renzulli (guitar), Antonio Aiazzi (keyboards), Gianni Maroccolo (bass), and Francesco Calamai (drums). The name is an acronym for **L**ocalità **IT**alia **FI**renze via dei **BAr**di (their rehearsal room's telex address: LIT-FI-BA also interpreted as LITteratura, FIloSofia, BAr).
- First live show: **6 December 1980** at **Rockoteca Brighton**, Settignano (Florence suburb) — a bootleg of this concert exists and is historically significant.
- Championed the idea of **"rock italiano cantato in italiano"** (Italian rock sung in Italian), writing most of the band's lyrics. His theatrical, confrontational stage presence became a defining characteristic.
- Litfiba evolved from new wave/post-punk into hard rock, incorporating Mediterranean/Latin/worldbeat elements.
- **Trilogia del Potere** (Trilogy of Power): Desaparecido (1985), 17 Re (1986), and Litfiba 3 (1988) — a thematic trilogy rejecting totalitarianism.
- Commercial peak with **El Diablo** (1990, Platinum), **Terremoto** (1993), and **Infinito** (1999, 1 million copies sold).
- Left Litfiba in **July 1999** amid tensions with Renzulli, shortly after the Infinito tour.

### Solo Career (1999–present)

- **1999**: Released anti-war single **"Il mio nome è mai più"** with Ligabue and Jovanotti (proceeds donated to Emergency). Sold 400,000+ copies.
- **2000**: Debut solo album **"Né buoni né cattivi"** (Sony Music Italy). Hit singles "Toro Loco" and "Bomba Boomerang." Peaked at #4 FIMI, sold 100,000+ copies.
- **2002**: **"U.D.S. – L'uomo della strada"** — duet **"L'amore immaginato"** with Anggun reached #1 on Italian Singles Chart.
- **2004**: **"Soggetti smarriti"** — introspective, alternative rock.
- **2006**: **"In faccia"** — return to raw rock sound. Lead single "Tribù" presented at 1st May Concert in Rome.
- **2007**: **"Storytellers"** — live album.
- **2008**: **"Fenomeni"** — compilation of re-recorded solo + Litfiba material.
- **2010 & 2013**: Reunited with Litfiba for reunion tours (Stato Libero di Litfiba tour, Trilogia del Potere live).
- **2013**: **"Identikit"** — studio album.
- **2020**: Competed at **Sanremo Music Festival 2020** with **"Pugili fragili"** (finished 8th).
- **2020**: **"Pugili fragili"** album — inspired by Sanremo entry.
- **2021**: Published autobiography **"Spacca l'infinito. Il romanzo di una vita"** (Giunti).
- **October 2022**: Suffered a collapse during a recording session resulting in permanent acoustic nerve damage. Documented in the 2025 film *Piero Pelù. Rumore dentro* (premiered at Venice Film Festival).
- **2024**: **"Deserti"** — latest studio album. Singles: "Novichok," "Maledetto Cuore."

## Underground Connections & Scene Affiliations

### Florence New Wave / Dark Wave Scene (1980s)

Litfiba was at the epicenter of the **Florentine new wave / post-punk / dark wave scene** of the early-to-mid 1980s, alongside:

| Band | Genre | Connection to Litfiba/Pelù |
|------|-------|---------------------------|
| **Diaframma** (id=98) | Post-punk / Dark wave | Recorded **Amsterdam EP** together (1985). Federico Fiumani and Piero Pelù shared a close friendship. Both bands founded almost simultaneously in Florence. Pankow and Diaframma's Federico Fiumani attended Litfiba's first concert (6 Dec 1980). Diaframma contributed members to Litfiba's orbit (Ringo de Palma played in Mugnions → Litfiba → CCCP). |
| **Pankow** (id=99) | Post-punk / New wave | Fellow Florentine scene contemporaries. Federico Fiumani (Diaframma) collaborated with Pankow. Shared bills and underground network. |
| **Neon** | Dark wave / Electronic | Formed 1979 in Florence. Part of same dark wave/electronic scene. Marcello Michelotti's project. |
| **Moda** | New wave | Co-contributed to **Catalogue Issue** compilation (1984) with Litfiba and Diaframma on I.R.A. Records. |
| **Underground Life** | New wave | Also on **Catalogue Issue** compilation. |
| **Polyactive** | New wave | Florentine scene band. |
| **Karnak** | New wave | Florentine scene band. |

### Key Scene Hubs & Events

- **Rockoteca Brighton** (Settignano, Florence): Litfiba's debut venue (6 Dec 1980). A community hall (casa del popolo) that became the hub for Florence's alternative music scene.
- **Manila** (Florence): Venue for the famous 1983 bootleg recording.
- **Festa dell'Unità** (Campi Bisenzio, Florence): Litfiba performed here in 1988 — important left-wing cultural event in Italy.
- **Suono Records** (Mestre): Label that released *Italia Wiva Complication 1* (1983) and *Eneide di Krypton* (1983), key documentations of the early underground scene.
- **I.R.A. Records** (Florence): Founded by producer **Alberto Pirelli** specifically to release Litfiba's *Desaparecido*. Released the *Catalogue Issue* compilation (1984) uniting Litfiba, Diaframma, Moda, Underground Life.
- **Contempo Records**: Released *Yassassin* EP (1984), reissued *Amsterdam* EP (2018).

### Contextual Note on the Demotape DB Entry

The entry "Piero Pelù" (id=97) likely represents the solo artist as distinct from Litfiba (id=95). In Italian underground music documentation, it's common to list the frontman separately when his solo work takes on a different character from the band. Pelù's solo work, while more commercially oriented, retains underground credibility through:
- His continued advocacy for Italian-language rock
- Collaborations with underground figures (Diaframma, CCCP members, Saverio Lanza)
- The raw, political nature of his lyrics
- His activism (Emergency NGO, anti-war work)

The connection to RockGalileo (id=86) is well-documented: Saverio Lanza (RockGalileo) collaborated with Pelù on solo projects, and Gianmarco Colzi (RockGalileo) later became Litfiba's drummer (2001–2006).
