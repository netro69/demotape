## Overview

Disciplinatha was an Italian industrial rock / punk rock band formed in 1987 in Bentivoglio, province of Bologna, Emilia-Romagna. They were known for an eccentric and aggressive fusion of industrial, electronic, post-punk, and punk rock, combined with provocative, politically-charged iconography and lashing lyrics. Active for a decade, they released two studio albums, two EPs, and a major box set anthology. After disbanding in 1997, the core members reformed as **Dish-Is-Nein** in 2017. Founding guitarist Dario Parisini died on June 9, 2022, aged 56.

## History & Active Years

### Formation & Early Period (1987–1991)

Disciplinatha was formed in 1987 by Dario Parisini (guitar), Cristiano Santini (vocals/guitar), Marco Maiani (bass), and Daniele Albertazzi (drums). Parisini and Santini had previously played together in a band called "Blood" with Maiani. The name "Disciplinatha" itself was a neologism blending "discipline" with a suffix evoking religious/mystical connotations.

They entered the scene with a repertoire of lashing lyrics grafted onto energetic rock that fused industrial, electronic, and post-punk. They were produced by **Jumpy Velena** (Helena Velena), a transgender activist and producer who founded **Attack Punk Records** in Bologna in 1981 — the same label that released the early records of CCCP Fedeli alla linea.

### Breakthrough & Controversy (1988–1993)

- **1988**: Debut EP *Abbiamo Pazientato 40 Anni: Ora Basta!* on Attack Punk Records. The title quotes a Mussolini speech, the cover features Balilla (fascist youth organization imagery), yet it was released by the most radical and anarchic hardcore label in Italy — a deliberate, intelligent provocation. Parisini later described it as "an unrepeatable splinter made without any instrumental or technical ability."
- **1991**: Second EP *Crisi di Valori* ("Crisis of Values"). The title track samples the voice of Pope John Paul II. The EP bore the polemical statement: "Non siamo di destra, anzi, siamo buoni" ("We are not right-wing, on the contrary, we are good").
- **1992**: Performed at the historic concert at Centro per l'arte contemporanea Luigi Pecci, Prato (September 18), alongside C.S.I. and Üstmamò. This was C.S.I.'s debut concert after the dissolution of CCCP Fedeli alla linea.
- **1993**: The Prato concert was documented on the live compilation *Maciste contro tutti*, shared by C.S.I., Disciplinatha, and Üstmamò.

### Studio Albums (1994–1997)

- **1994**: *Un Mondo Nuovo* — first studio album, produced by CPI. Introduced samples, electronic drums, and programmed parts. Marked evolution from raw punk toward more textured, layered sound.
- **1995**: *A Raccolta* — limited CD compilation integrating the debut EP with rarities and previously unreleased live versions.
- **1996**: *Primigenia* — final studio album, released July 11, 1996. Produced by CPI. Considered more aligned with its era compared to the raw, unrepeatable energy of the debut. Rolling Stone noted the band had gone from being "feared terrorists with a bounty on their heads" to "beloved rockstars more or less by everyone" in just a few years.
- **1997**: Band officially disbanded.

### Post-Breakup & Reformation as Dish-Is-Nein (2012–present)

- **2012**: *Tesori della Patria* — monumental box set (3xLP + 12" + 12" + DVD) released by Black Fading Records/Contempo Records, containing the entire studio discography on vinyl. The release event featured an impromptu reunion concert.
- **2017**: Core members Cristiano Santini, Roberta Vicinelli, and Valeria Cevolani reformed as **Dish-Is-Nein** (a pun: "Dish Is Nein" = "This is no" in German-sounding Italian; also readable as "This is none" / a wordplay on "Disciplinatha").
- **2018**: Dish-Is-Nein self-titled EP released on Contempo Records. Featured **Justin Bennett** (drummer for Skinny Puppy, Peter Murphy, Rozz Williams) on drums.
- **2022**: Dario Parisini died on June 9 after a long illness, aged 56. Tributes poured in from across the Italian music world.
- **2023**: Dish-Is-Nein released 7" single *The Metal Machine* on Black Fading Records, featuring a remixed version of the track "Eva."
- **2025**: Dish-Is-Nein released LP *Occidente (A Funeral Party)* on Black Fading Records, hailed by critics as one of the best albums of 2025. Justin Bennett on drums.

## Interesting Facts

1. **Provocative debut**: The 1988 EP title *Abbiamo Pazientato 40 Anni: Ora Basta!* ("We've Patiently Waited 40 Years: Now Enough!") quotes a Mussolini speech, and the cover depicts Balilla (fascist youth). Yet it was released by the most radically anarchist hardcore label in Italy — a deliberate, intelligent political provocation rather than genuine fascism.

2. **Papal sampling**: The 1991 EP *Crisi di Valori* sampled the voice of Pope John Paul II in its title track — a bold act of cultural subversion in a predominantly Catholic country.

3. **"Emilia's rock dam"**: Rolling Stone Italia described them as "l'argine rock all'Emilia rossa" — the rock dam holding back the dominance of left-wing cultural hegemony in Emilia-Romagna, one of Italy's most politically "red" (left-wing) regions.

4. **Iconic live performances**: Their live shows featured extraordinary guest performers: the Alpine Choir of Monte Calisio, Mondine (female rice paddy workers from Bentivoglio), and a choir of Alpini (Italian Alpine troops), creating a unique spectacle blending militarism, folk tradition, and industrial noise.

5. **Dario Parisini's death**: Founding guitarist and "soul" of the band died June 9, 2022, aged 56, after a long illness. The Dish-Is-Nein 12" single *The Metal Machine* was dedicated to him as "the end of a cycle."

6. **Dish-Is-Nein name**: The successor band's name is a triple pun: "Dish Is Nein" sounds like German for "This is no," resembles "This is none" in English, and phonetically evokes "Disciplinatha" when read in Italian.

7. **Critical reappraisal**: While their debut EP was raw and technically unrefined (Parisini himself called it "an unrepeatable splinter"), their final album *Primigenia* (1996) was more aligned with its era. Parisini noted the debut "has aged much better than Primigenia."

8. **Continued relevance**: Dish-Is-Nein's 2025 album *Occidente (A Funeral Party)* was hailed by critics as one of the best albums of that year, proving the project's enduring creative vitality 38+ years after Disciplinatha's formation.
