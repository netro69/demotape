About Demotape

A living archive of Italian underground music. Every independent production — vinyl, CD, demotape — that was pressed by bands on their own labels, catalogued and preserved in digital space before it disappears forever.

The Problem

Italian underground/alternative bands have been producing music independently for decades. They press vinyl, burn CDs, record demotapes — all on their own labels, with no distribution network. These productions exist in basements, attics, and a few collectors' hands. No major catalogue (Discogs, MusicBrainz, etc.) has most of them. When the physical copies deteriorate or the collectors pass away, this music is gone.

What We Preserve

  • Band metadata — name, city, active years, members, genre
  • Release metadata — title, format, year, label, catalog number
  • Band connections — shared members, scene peers, label mates, venues
  • Lost material — audio, lyrics, artwork that is out of print and unavailable anywhere
  • Source references — URLs, archive citations, reliability ratings

What We Don't Do

  • We don't host currently available commercial releases — we link to legitimate sources
  • We don't replace a commercial release with a pirated copy
  • We don't host full tracklists of copyrighted material

The Mission

Most of these bands are no longer active. Their catalogs are "lost" — out of print, not digitized, existing only in physical copies in collectors' hands. Demotape preserves what remains: the metadata, the connections, and the truly lost material that would otherwise vanish forever.

How It Works

We crawl from band to band, starting with community forums (RadioChitarra, PunkMI), cross-referencing Discogs and specialized databases, and documenting the connections between bands, labels, and venues. Each band leads to more bands — the social graph of Italian underground music.

Contact
