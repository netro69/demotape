## Bands & Projects

### M.T.T. (Solo Project, 1985–)
Teardo's solo experimental/industrial project. Started with cassette releases on his own M.T.T. Records in Pordenone.

### Meathead (Founding member, 1991/1992–1997)
Industrial rock band. Lineup: Teho Teardo (vocals, guitar, sampler), Deison (sampler, vocals), G.No (bass), Matt/Nicky (drums), Guana (sampler). Produced by Paolo Favati (Pankow). Released 4 albums. Collaborated with Pain Teens, Zeni Geva, Cop Shoot Cop, Babyland.

### Here (1996–1998+)
Electro-rock trio: Teho Teardo (vocals, sampler, guitar), Jim Filer Coleman (Cop Shoot Cop/Phylr – sampler, piano), Mick Harris (Napalm Death/Scorn). Also: Lydia Lunch (spoken voice/words on some recordings). Released *Brooklyn Bank* (1997) on Tzadik and EP *A Bit Of Red* (1998).

### Matera (with Mick Harris)
Experimental electronic project with Scorn's Mick Harris. Released *Same Here* (1996) on Invisible Records.

### Operator (with Scott McCloud, 2003)
Electronica/punk/pop project with Girls Against Boys guitarist. Released *Welcome To The Wonderful World* (2003, Remae label). Toured with Placebo.

### Modern Institute (with Martina Bertoni, 2006)
Duo with cellist Martina Bertoni. Released *Excellent Swimmer* (2006) on Expanding Records. Cinematic instrumental album combining cello, electronics, guitar, Rhodes. Guest: Mark Beazley (Rothko).

### Teho Teardo & Blixa Bargeld (2013–present)
Ongoing collaboration with Einstürzende Neubauten frontman. Four albums: *Still Smiling* (2013), *Nerissimo* (2016), *Christian & Mauro* (2024), plus live album *Live in Berlin* (2022). Also EPs *Spring* (2014) and *Fall* (2017). Label: Specula Records.

## Summary

Mauro Theo Teardo (Teho Teardo) is a **well-documented, traceable figure** in the Italian underground/industrial scene — not obscure at all. He has been active since 1985, first with cassette releases on his own M.T.T. Records, then with the industrial rock band Meathead (1992–1997), and later with numerous collaborative projects (Here, Matera, Operator, Modern Institute, and the ongoing Blixa Bargeld partnership).

The name "Mauro Theo Teardo" appears to be the full birth name (Mauro Teardo with "Theo" inserted as a middle name/prefix). All sources refer to him as Mauro Teardo / Teho Teardo. "Theo" comes from his childhood nickname "Teo" (schoolmates called him Teo), later stylized as "Teho."

**This artist is NOT suitable for the Demotape "obscure underground" profile** — he is extremely well-documented, has mainstream film score credits (Paolo Sorrentino's *Il Divo*, *The Family Friend*), has won a David di Donatello award, and has extensive presence on Discogs, Bandcamp, RateYourMusic, IMDb, and multiple music press outlets. His underground cassette-era work is well-preserved and accessible via Bandcamp and blogs.
