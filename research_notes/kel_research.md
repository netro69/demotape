---

## Background
KEL grew up between Sicily and Malta in a culturally diverse environment. She began singing at age 4 and started formal vocal training at age 8. Her Maltese-Sicilian roots combined with German influences shaped her musical identity. She describes herself as an "eclectic introvert" who bridges Mediterranean traditions with contemporary electronic sounds.
