## Biography

Enrico Gabrielli (born 24 October 1976, Montevarchi, Arezzo, Italy) is an Italian multi-instrumentalist, composer, arranger, producer, and writer. Also known by his pseudonym **Der Maurer** (German for "The Bricklayer"), he is a central node in Italy's experimental/library/psychedelic underground.

A graduate of the Conservatorio G. Verdi di Milano (clarinet, composition), he studied under Richard Stoltzman, Antony Pay, Ciro Scarponi, Danilo Lorenzini, and Salvatore Sciarrino. In 1996, he founded the Ensemble Niccolò Castiglioni (later Ensemble Risognanze). He played for three years at the Tiroler Festspiele in Erl.

He has contributed to over **250 recordings** in a 25+ year career. His work spans psychedelic funk, chamber composition, film/TV scoring, children's music, and literature. He is also part of the Milanese cabaret group **I Calimari** with Dente, Gianluca De Rubertis, Federico Dragogna, and Filippo Cecconi.

## Core Bands & Projects

### Calibro 35 (2007–present)
**Founding member** — keyboards, saxophone, bass clarinet, flute
- Cinematic funk/jazz-rock band formed in Milan in 2007
- Music sampled by **Dr. Dre** ("One Shot One Kill"), **Jay-Z** ("Picasso Baby"), **Damon Albarn**, **Demigodz**
- Performed at **Milano-Cortina 2026 Winter Olympics** closing ceremony
- 11+ studio albums including film/TV scores (Blanca, Sandokan, Diabolik)

### The Winstons (2015/2016–present)
**Founding member** as "Enro Winston" — keyboards, woodwinds, vocals
- Progressive rock/Canterbury scene trio
- Members: Lino Gitto (Linnon), Roberto Dell'Era (Rob, Afterhours/Le Vibrazioni)
- 3 studio albums (2016, 2020, 2024)

### 19'40" (2016–present)
**Co-founder** with Sebastiano De Gennaro, Francesco Fusaro
- Recording series / label: subscription CD series, one release every 3 months
- 14+ releases. "Anti-classical" concept bridging composed music and underground aesthetics
- Direct parallel to demotape culture ethos

### Mariposa (c. 2000)
**Founding member**
- Early Italian psychedelic funk project
- Debut album: *The Ol' Dirt Road* (2000)

### Esecutori di Metallo su Carta (2016–present)
**Co-founder** with Sebastiano De Gennaro
- Contemporary/experimental ensemble
- Released *Progetto Generativo* — reworkings of Italian underground artists: Zu, Aucan, Morkobot, Julie's Haircut, Hobocombo

### Other Projects

## Note on Project Scope
Enrico Gabrielli's active career (2000–present) falls partly **outside the project's 1985–2000 core timeline**. However, his aesthetic universe — vintage synths, analog recording, Italian library music revival, small-batch physical releases, and bridging of high-concept composition with underground aesthetics — and his deep connections to underground artists make him highly relevant. The **19'40" label** operates on a subscription model that directly mirrors demotape culture.
