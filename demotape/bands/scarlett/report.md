## Origin

**Kingston upon Hull, East Riding of Yorkshire, England**

Cheryl Parker and Jo Youle met at Wolfreton School in Hull. They initially formed a trio called **Cheap Day Return** (with bassist Ed Clark) in 1989, playing Hull pubs for two years. The name was borrowed from a Jethro Tull song.

---

## Notable Facts

- **"Independent Love Song"** was their biggest hit (#12 UK, Top 10 Ireland, Feb 1995) — a power ballad with orchestral string arrangements by Anne Dudley
- Featured in the film *Bed of Roses* and TV series *Derry Girls*
- Performed on **BBC's A Song for Europe 1995** (UK Eurovision selection show)
- Supported **Bryan Ferry** on UK tour
- Band disbanded circa 1997 after *Chemistry* failed to chart and WEA dropped them
- **Database anomaly:** The DB connection links Scarlett to SRNDE as a vocalist on "Up To The Top" (2025), suggesting the original import may have been a different artist entirely — not the 1990s British duo
